Privacy Policy

Brevis Publisher is used only by its owner, the artist Brevis.

  • What it keeps: the sign-in tokens for the owner's own TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts, his planned posts, and the view and like counts of what he posted. These stay encrypted on the owner's own computers.
  • What it does not do: it does not collect, sell or share anyone's personal data, and it does not read other people's accounts.
  • Where data goes: only to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, to post the owner's content and read its numbers.
  • YouTube: Brevis Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to his own channel and read their view, like and comment counts. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removal: the owner can disconnect an account at any time, which deletes its tokens; access can also be removed in the TikTok app settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions for YouTube.

Contact: Brevis on Instagram @brevismusic.
